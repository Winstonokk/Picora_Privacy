Privacy Policy

Picora is a fully offline, local-first photo collage maker for Android. Your photos stay on your device. No account. No ads.

Last updated: 12 November 2026 (v1.1 — added Firebase Analytics disclosure) — added Firebase Analytics disclosure) · Product: Picora for Android
Local-first by design: Your photos and collages are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded by us. The App uses Google Analytics for Firebase (anonymous usage statistics) and Google AdMob (advertising) — never photo content or any data that identifies you personally.

1. Information We Collect

Picora has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no server-side storage. Everything you create stays on your device. The only data leaving your device is anonymous usage analytics described in §3.

Photos you select

  • The App requests read access to your device photo library so you can pick photos to include in a collage.
  • Photos you choose are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded anywhere.
  • We do not access or use any photo you have not explicitly selected.

Collages you create

  • Exported collages are saved to your device gallery (Pictures/Picora) via the system MediaStore.
  • They remain on your device; we have no access to them.

2. Permissions

PermissionPurpose
READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGEDisplay your photo library inside the in-app picker so you can choose photos.
INTERNETReserved for future features. The current version makes no network requests.
CAMERA (optional)Take a new photo from the picker's Camera tab, if you choose.

3. Third-Party Services

Picora integrates two Google services (Google LLC) to keep the App free:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase — anonymous usage statistics: app-instance IDs, screen views (home / editor / settings), aggregated events (template selected, export saved, tool used) and crash reports. Photo/collage content is never collected. Analytics is configured without ad personalization signals.
  • Google AdMob — serves Banner ads (home, editor) and optional Rewarded ads (remove watermark on export). AdMob may use device identifiers such as the advertising ID to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads where you consent via Google's consent prompt.

Data from these services is processed by Google per the Firebase Privacy & Security and Google Ads policies. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in Android Settings → Google → Ads.

4. Children's Privacy

Picora does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because no data is collected at all, the App is compliant with children's privacy regulations (COPPA, GDPR-K).

5. Data Storage & Retention

All data (selected photos, exported collages, preferences) lives only on your device. Uninstalling the App removes its preferences; collages you exported remain in your gallery unless you delete them.

6. Your Rights & Choices

  • Revoke photo access anytime in Android Settings → Apps → Picora → Permissions. The App shows a permission guide and stays usable.
  • Delete exported collages from your gallery app at any time.

7. Changes to This Policy

The "Last updated" date above reflects the current version. Material changes will be highlighted in the App's release notes on Google Play.
