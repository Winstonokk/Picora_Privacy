Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Picora handles your information when you use our Android application, and outlines the rights and choices available to you.

Last updated: 12 November 2026 · Product: Picora for Android · Developer: Winstonokk
Summary: Your photos and collages are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded by us. The App collects only anonymous usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase) and serves advertising (Google AdMob). We never collect the content of your photos or collages.

1. Introduction

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy ("Policy") describes how Picora ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information in connection with the Picora Android application (the "App") and explains the rights and choices available to you.

By downloading, accessing, or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Policy. If you do not agree with this Policy, please do not use the App.

2. Collection of Information and Processing Purposes

2.1 Information Processed Entirely on Your Device

The core functionality of Picora is processing photos locally. The following data is accessed and processed on your device only, and is never transmitted to us or to any third party:

  • Photos you select from your device library to include in a collage. They are read solely for the purpose of composing the collage you request.
  • Collages you create and export. Exported images are saved to your device gallery via the system MediaStore (Pictures/Picora).
  • Photos taken with the camera through the App's picker. They are stored in local cache and placed into your collage.
  • App preferences (e.g., selected language) stored locally via Android DataStore/SharedPreferences.

We do not access, read, or use any photo you have not explicitly selected, and we do not create copies of your library.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically (Usage Data)

The App integrates Google Analytics for Firebase (Google LLC) to collect anonymous usage statistics that help us understand which features are used and to detect crashes. This includes:

  • Anonymous app-instance identifiers;
  • Screen views (home, editor, settings) and aggregated in-app events (template selected, layout switched, tool used, photos picked, export saved, rewarded ad shown);
  • Device model, OS version, locale, and approximate device configuration;
  • Crash and diagnostic reports.

This data does not include the content of your photos or collages, file names, or any information that directly identifies you. Analytics is configured without ad personalization signals.

2.3 Advertising Data

The App integrates Google AdMob (Google LLC) to serve Banner advertisements on the home and editor screens and optional Rewarded video advertisements (e.g., to remove the watermark on export). AdMob may process device identifiers — including the Google advertising ID — device information, and approximate location data (derived from IP address) to serve and measure advertisements. Where you consent through Google's consent prompt, advertising may be personalized; you may withdraw consent or reset your advertising ID at any time (see Section 8).

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Information is shared only with the following service providers, solely as described in this Policy:

  • Google LLC — Google Analytics for Firebase (usage statistics, crash reporting) and Google AdMob (advertising). Data is processed by Google per the Firebase Privacy & Security and Google Ads policies.
  • Google Play — when you use the in-app review prompt or open the store listing, Google Play processes the interaction under its own terms.

We may also disclose information if required by law, regulation, or valid legal process.

5. Storage of Information

Photos, collages, and app preferences are stored locally on your device. Anonymous analytics and advertising data are stored and processed by Google on servers that may be located outside your country of residence, in accordance with Google's certifications under the EU–US and Swiss–US Data Privacy Frameworks.

Uninstalling the App removes its local preferences. Collages you exported remain in your device gallery unless you delete them.

6. Security Measures

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including processing photos in local memory only, transmitting analytics over encrypted connections (TLS), requesting runtime permissions rather than broad access, and integrating third-party services in read-only, minimal-disclosure configurations.

7. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or the higher minimum age required by your local law). Because the App processes photos only locally and collects only anonymous statistics, it is designed to be compliant with children's privacy regulations, including COPPA and GDPR-K.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

8. Your Rights (GDPR and Other Regions)

Depending on your jurisdiction (including the EU/EEA under GDPR, the UK, and California under CCPA/CPRA), you may have the following rights with respect to personal data processed about you:

  • Right of access and information about the data processed;
  • Right to rectification of inaccurate data;
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten");
  • Right to restrict or object to processing;
  • Right to data portability;
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time (without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing);
  • Right not to be discriminated against for exercising your rights (California).

Because Picora processes no information that directly identifies you, most requests will relate to the anonymous analytics and advertising identifiers described above. To exercise these rights, contact us at wangfengkxhp@gmail.com. You may also:

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads;
  • Opt out of ad personalization in the same location;
  • Request deletion of your Firebase app-instance data via Google's user deletion tools;
  • Uninstall the App, which removes all locally stored preferences.

California residents may additionally learn more about ad personalization and opt-out choices at Google's ad settings page. We do not "sell" or "share" personal information as defined by the CCPA/CPRA.
